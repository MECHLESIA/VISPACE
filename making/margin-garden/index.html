Margin Garden

A page is never quite empty after someone has loved it.

Open the marks beside these lines. Someone has left you a little room.

01

The path was made by people
who were not looking for a path.

A pencilled arrow

It points away from the sentence, toward the blank space. Perhaps the reader had somewhere else to go. Perhaps this was their way of taking us.

At the edge of the page
02

Under the crossed-out word,
another season.

A word written twice

Someone wrote “stay,” then softened it to “rest.” The difference is small enough to fit beneath a fingernail. Large enough to make a door.

In a different shade of ink
03

Leave something unfinished.
Not a wound. A window.

A folded corner

This corner holds no answer. Only the place where a reader stopped, and trusted that the world would let them return.

No date, no signature
A margin of your own

What would you leave
for an unknown reader?

A direction. A kindness. A sentence that does not need to explain itself.
